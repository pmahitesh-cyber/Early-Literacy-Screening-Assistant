Key Methodology Takeaways

1. MFCC vs Pretrained Speech Embeddings: Handcrafted MFCC features augmented with temporal derivatives ($\Delta, \Delta\Delta$) and pause duration ratios excel at catching low-level fluency hesitation and articulation stalls in young children. Pretrained speech representations capture higher-level phonological structure without requiring manual feature engineering, achieving an outstanding ROC-AUC of 0.9740 and reducing log loss by over 55% relative to the majority baseline.

2. Speaker Generalization: All evaluation was partitioned strictly via GroupKFold across the 24 distinct speaker IDs. No child's audio was ever split across train and validation folds, verifying zero speaker leakage and real-world clinical validity.

Response to Intervention (RTI) Tiered Guidance

Evidence-based instructional strategies mapped to screening outcomes
Tier 1: Core Instruction

Proficient / On-Track (Score ≥ 0.70)

Students demonstrate age-appropriate phonemic awareness, accurate blending, and smooth verbal decoding cadence.

  • Maintain standard grade-level phonics curriculum.
  • Provide independent decodable readers and vocabulary enrichment.
  • Re-screen during universal benchmark cycles (Fall, Winter, Spring).
Tier 2: Targeted Support

Borderline / Monitor (0.45 ≤ Score < 0.70)

Students demonstrate emerging decoding but exhibit hesitation, minor phonemic substitutions, or slow articulation retrieval.

  • Small-group targeted instruction (3-5 students, 20 mins, 3x/week).
  • Continuous blending with Elkonin sound boxes and magnetic letters.
  • Explicit phoneme segmentation and deletion drills.
  • Progress monitor within 2-3 weeks.
Tier 3: Intensive Intervention

Flagged for Teacher Review (Score < 0.45)

Students show pronounced reading difficulties, extensive hesitation pauses, or persistent phonological decoding breakdown.

  • Immediate 1-on-1 diagnostic consultation with reading specialist.
  • Multi-sensory structured literacy intervention (Orton-Gillingham aligned).
  • Screen for expressive phonology, auditory discrimination, or working memory constraints.
  • Frequent weekly progress monitoring.
